# StoryNest Product Design

## Strategy
StoryNest is a premium long-form reading application. It focuses on high-quality storytelling, sophisticated aesthetics, and user engagement through gamification (streaks) and social interaction.

## Brand
- **Voice:** Warm, inviting, and professional. We are the "Nest" for imagination.
- **Tone:** Sophisticated but accessible.
- **Anti-references:** Generic SaaS dashboards, loud neon crypto apps, cluttered news feeds.

## Users
- Passionate readers who value focus and beauty.
- Authors looking for a high-end platform to showcase their work.
- Admins who need simple, powerful content management tools.
